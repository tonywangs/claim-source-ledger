TELEGRAMS.
Heart's Content, July 29th.
The Bloodhound arrived here at five this morning. She left again at three, with new Cable on board for the repair of the Gulf line.
The Osprey arrived here at three p.m. to-day.
The Great Eastern will remain here this week, and then proceed to pick up the old Atlantic Cable.
Mr. Mackay left here on Friday night in H. M. S. Niger for Cape Breton with despatches from the Queen to the President.
He will also superintend the repairing of the Gulf Cable.

Postscript.
We have great pleasure in laying before our readers the following very pleasing telegram, as received by our local government yesterday:—
“Heart's Content, 31st July.
Queen's message has been delivered to the President. Reply has been sent through the Cable to the Queen. Great Eastern is now firing a salute.
CYRUS W. FIELD.”
